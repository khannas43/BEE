# WP04.2b-2b — Closing and revoking register entries by a second person (R1)

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `wp04-register-revocation` (14 October 2026). **Not accepted.** WP04 stays open at 1/11.

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** Owner defaults accepted 14 October 2026 ([WP04.2b_PLAN.md](WP04.2b_PLAN.md)): a registered brand or an agency authorisation is closed by two people.

## What exists

| Part | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Roles | **Programme** proposes (capability `register_revoke_propose`); the **Director** approves or rejects (`register_revoke_decide`); the proposer may withdraw. Nobody decides their own proposal (service and database). |
| What can be proposed | **Revoke a brand**: the brand becomes revoked and every active authorisation for it is revoked with it. **Revoke an agency authorisation.** **Close an agency authorisation on a date**: it no longer applies from that date; the date is not before today, after the authorisation's start and before its end. A brand can only be revoked (suspension is BL-182). |
| One at a time | A record has at most one open proposal (`revocation_pending`); a record that is no longer active cannot be proposed again (`target_not_active`). A rejected or withdrawn proposal frees it. |
| Approval | One database function (`register_revocation_decide`, V46) re-checks the record and the date, and changes the register in the same step; a refusal changes nothing (`target_not_active`, `closing_date_passed`). The runtime login can neither update a proposal nor write the register. |
| Effect on the model flow | None needed: filing already refuses a revoked brand or authorisation (`brand_not_permitted`, WP04.2a) and `eligible-brands` no longer offers it. A closed authorisation works until its closing date. Applications already filed are not touched (BL-183). |
| Routes | `GET /api/register-revocations` (the register entries and the proposals; Programme and the Director), `POST /api/register-revocations` (propose), `POST /api/register-revocations/{id}/decision` (`approve`, `reject` with a note, `withdraw`). Contract **0.30.0**; new codes `revocation_pending`, `target_not_active`, `closing_date_passed`. |
