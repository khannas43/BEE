# S4: quarterly production submission (local demonstration)

- **Real:** Spring module `production` (`/api/demo/production`), migrations V59-V60, role and organisation scope, state machine (draft, submitted, accepted, query), bulk CSV validation against issued certificates, SHA-256 of CA evidence, audit trail (`production_event`), segregation of the reviewer.
- **Simulated / not verified:** CA evidence is stored and hashed, never parsed or checked ("recorded, not verified"). Rules (units 1 to 10,000,000, dates inside the quarter, one submission per organisation and quarter, 10 percent reconciliation margin in `production_setting`) are provisional, not BEE rules. Reconciliation flags are prompts for a person; nothing is decided automatically.
- **Seed:** `local/seed/demo/s4_production.sql` creates approved NOVA models (BEE/RAC/2026/10101 to 10103) and a PIXEL model (10104) with certificates, and four NOVA quarters: 2025-Q4, 2026-Q1, 2026-Q2 accepted; 2026-Q3 submitted. **2026-Q3 has a spike: NC-RAC-12D declares 21,500 units in September against about 4,300 in other months**, for the AI anomaly use case (S6) to find. 2026-Q4 is left free for the live demo.
- **Shared tables:** `production_submission(id, organisation_id, quarter, state)`, `production_row(submission_id, model_application_id, units)` plus `period_start`, `period_end`, `registration_id`.
- **Demo:** (1) nova.applicant: create 2026-Q4; paste the sample CSV with a bad line, press "Validate (dry run)", see the per-row errors; fix, store; attach evidence with CA details; submit; editing is now closed. (2) bee.programme: open the 2026-Q3 or the new submission, read the reconciliation (shows "QR data not available" until S2 serials exist; then flags models whose declared units exceed bound serials by more than the margin), accept or raise a query with a note. (3) nova.applicant sees the query, edits, resubmits. nova.applicant cannot review; a reviewer from the submitting organisation is refused (segregation).
- **Routes:** GET/POST `production`; GET `production/{id}`, `/reconciliation[?margin=]`, `/evidence[?content=1]`; POST `production/{id}/rows|evidence|submit|review`. Rows: `csv`, or `rows` array, or one row; `commit` false (default for csv/rows) is a dry run, true is all or nothing; a single row commits by default.
