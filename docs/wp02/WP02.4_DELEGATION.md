# WP02.4c — Delegation of a stage role (14 October 2026)

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** These are the owner's defaults, taken from the candidate rules in [WP02.3_MFA.md](WP02.3_MFA.md) §7, which BEE has not confirmed. D1 stands: the Secretary's final approval is not delegable.

## What exists

| Part | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| What can be delegated | **Programme, Director and Finance** (roles held for all records that act at a stage). Never the Secretary, never an administrative role, never an applicant role, never a role tied to an assignment (IAME, Reviewer). The delegate gets that role for all records, as the delegator has it. |
| Who asks | The holder of the role (the delegator) names a colleague: an active account with a role of their own and an organisation in common. Dates: start today or later, at most 30 days. A reason is required. |
| Who approves | Three different people: the delegator asks, a person who holds the permission `delegation_approve` (the Administrator) approves or rejects (a reason is needed to reject), and the delegate is the third. The database function refuses the delegator and the delegate as approver, and re-checks at approval that the period has not ended, the delegator still holds the role as an active account, and the delegate is still eligible. |
| Withdraw, revoke | The delegator withdraws a request that is still waiting. The delegator or an approver revokes an approved delegation; it ends on the next request. No delegation is open-ended; expiry is checked against server time on every request (the day it names is the first day it no longer applies). |
| What it changes | For the delegate, `Caller.roles()` includes the delegated role, so the model-application stage steps and the read scope work as for the delegator (`SlicePolicy`, the return and reject rules). It does **not** reach administrative capabilities (fee rules, rating schemes, business settings, fee corrections, delegation approval), registration review, register revocation, or any database function that reads the person's own role assignments: those use the caller's own roles only. |
| Segregation of duties | A delegate who acts for a delegator is treated as that delegator too: if the delegator already acted at another stage of the application, the delegate is refused (403 `segregation_refused`). The delegate's own earlier steps count as before. |
| Audit | `delegation_event` (requested, approved, rejected, withdrawn, revoked: who, when, note) and `delegation_use` (every successful write that passed a role check only because of the delegation: the acting person, the delegator, the role, route, status, correlation id) are append-only. The history of a model application names the person who acted, not the delegator. The register of delegations is readable by the people concerned and, for an approver, in full (`GET /api/delegations`). |
| Routes | `GET /api/delegations`, `POST /api/delegations`, `POST /api/delegations/{id}/decision` (`approve`, `reject`, `withdraw`, `revoke`). Contract **0.32.0**; new codes `delegate_not_eligible`, `delegation_exists`, `delegation_not_active`. |

## Evidence

- `DelegationDatabaseTest` (3, real PostgreSQL, restricted runtime login): who may delegate what, the period limits, the three-person rule in the service and the database, the role held by the delegate and not more, the end of the period, the delegator losing the role, revocation, the trail and its immutability, the use log, segregation counting the delegator.
- `SpringContractTest.delegationDocumentedPairs` (every documented Spring status and code); request-log unit evidence for the three browser pairs.
- `npm run local:delegation` (`delegation-browser-check.cjs`): 106 checks over two runs, as real roles through the portal. The Director delegates the Director role to a Finance colleague; the Administrator approves; the delegate reads the Director stage, returns an application there (200), the use is logged and the history names the delegate; the same delegate is refused where the delegator already acted (segregation); after revocation the delegate cannot act or read the stage. Every row it makes is removed.
- The full `local:check` and the independent review are added below when run.
