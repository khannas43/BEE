Dispositions: **retain** is an entry route; **merge** folds into another route; **contextual** becomes a tab, panel or action inside a retained route; **retire** is removed in favour of the target; **dev-only** is hidden outside development.

Internal capacity is the Annex A.1 cell unless the row appears under Annex A.1 deviations. Partner capacity is proposed here: DDD Annex A.2 gives all five partner roles identical cells on every row, so it cannot be used unchanged.
