# WP09.1b — The printable certificate and label, with a QR code

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `wp09-certificate-print`; the full local gate passes (see Evidence). **Not independently reviewed or accepted.**

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner's defaults D7 to D9 ([BEE_DECISIONS.md](../BEE_DECISIONS.md), [WP09_PLAN.md](WP09_PLAN.md)): the certificate and the label are a printable web page with a QR image; the browser's Print gives the PDF. Both are marked **local demonstration, not issued by BEE, no legal effect**, and the label shows no annual-energy figure (no rule for it) and none of BEE's artwork (not available).

## What it does

`/app/model-label/label-preview` (the existing "Labels and certificates" entry, now real) lists the applicant's **approved** applications; opening one (`?id=…`) shows two documents and a "Print or save as PDF" button:

| Document | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of registration | The registration ID, the manufacturer, brand and model, the category, the star rating and efficiency figure, the validity and its status, the rating scheme, and a QR code. |
| Star label | The stars, the efficiency figure, brand and model, the registration ID, the validity, and the same QR code. |

The QR code is a real QR code (ISO 18004, byte mode, error-correction level M, from the `qrcode-generator` library) that points to `/verify?reg=<registration ID>` on the same address the page is served from. The next piece (WP09.1c) makes that page real. The dashboard's certificate card links here.

Printing hides the sidebar, the top bar, the identity strip and the buttons, and puts the label on its own page. An application that is not approved has no certificate, and says so.

## What changed

- **Dependencies:** `qrcode-generator` (runtime, no further dependencies) and `jsqr` (test only, to read a QR code back).
- **Web:** `lib/client/qr.ts` (matrix, SVG path, verification address), `QrCode` component, `CertificateAndLabel` (replaces the prototype `LabelPreviewScreen`), print rules in the console layout and `globals.css`, a runtime menu entry ("Certificate and label") for the applicant, a link on the dashboard card.
- **No backend or contract change:** the page reads the application detail read of WP09.1a.
- **Tests:** three QR tests that draw the code as pixels and decode it with a QR reader to prove it reads back as exactly the verification address (for three registration IDs, and for a longer address on another host); four tests of the screen; and in the Secretary live check (now 83 checks): the dashboard link, the list, the two documents, that both QR codes carry the verification address, and that print emulation shows only the documents.
