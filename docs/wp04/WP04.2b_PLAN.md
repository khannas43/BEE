# WP04.2b — Brand and agency registration (plan, 14 October 2026)

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner accepted these defaults on 14 October 2026. Requirement R1: manufacturers, traders or importers apply for agency and brand registration online, with documents and a fee, and approval runs as a workflow ([INVENTORY.md](../wp01/INVENTORY.md) R1). Nothing here is decided by BEE; the product shows it as provisional.

| # | Question | Default taken |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Who approves a registration? | Programme reviews and recommends; Director approves. The approver is never the submitter, and never the person who recommended. |
| 2 | Is there a registration fee? | Yes, through the existing fee-rule machinery, with a provisional amount marked as not a BEE rule. Finance confirms receipt as for a model fee. |
| 3 | Which documents are required? | One PDF (for example a trade licence), through the WP06.1a intake, 5 MiB limit unchanged. |

## Rules for every package

- Branch each `cursor/<task>` from the latest `main`; no applied Flyway migration is edited.
- Gate: tiered, per [the working agreement](../kit/AGENT_HANDOFF.md); a full `local:check` at the end of the group and after the migration.
- One independent review per activity. Accepted work packages stay 1/11 until the WP04 package review.

**Out of scope (to the backlog):** the real registration fee and documents (BEE), renewal of a registration, a public list of registered brands, organisation registration (the same pattern, later).
