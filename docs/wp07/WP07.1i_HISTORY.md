## What it does

`GET /api/model-applications/{id}/history` (browser route `/api/runtime/model-applications/{id}/history`): the ordered steps of one application, each joined to its own record.

| Rule | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Anyone who can read the application. The same read scope applies; an unknown or out-of-scope application is 404. |
| Officers | See every step with the actor's name, role and organisation, the note, and the step's facts (fee reference, finding, rating figures, reason). |
| The applicant | Sees the whole timeline in order, with the role and organisation of each step but **no personal names**. The five internal steps (IAME finding, Reviewer forward, rating, Director recommendation, Secretary approval) are marked `withheld`: no note, no facts. What was addressed to the applicant (their payment, the reason they were given, their own resubmission) is shown. |
| Order | By sequence 1..n, validated by the BFF. A re-rating keeps the earlier rating as version 1. |
