# WP04.2b-1 — Brand and agency registration: file, licence, fee (R1)

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `wp04-registration-1` (14 October 2026); gate evidence below. **Not independently reviewed or accepted.** WP04 stays open at 1/11. Review, approval and the register entry are WP04.2b-2.

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner accepted these defaults on 14 October 2026 ([WP04.2b_PLAN.md](WP04.2b_PLAN.md)): Programme reviews and Director approves (2b-2); a fee through the fee-rule machinery; one trade-licence PDF.

## What exists

| Part | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Request | A manufacturer files a **brand** request (a new name); an agency files an **agency** request for a manufacturer's active brand (start date, optional end). States: `draft`, `fee_due`, `programme_review` (built), then `director_approval`, `approved`, `returned`, `rejected` (2b-2). Reference `REG-<year>-<n>`. |
| Rules | A brand name is 1 to 120 safe characters, free of any registered brand and of any live request by the same manufacturer. One live request per agency and brand. An agency request needs a start date not before the day of submission; an end needs a start. |
| Licence | One PDF (`trade_licence`), immutable versions, the WP06.1a local store and limits (5 MiB). Upload, list and download; editable only while a draft. Stored as pending local verification; nothing claims it is genuine. |
| Submit | Needs the licence (`document_required`), the brand still free or active, and the fee the person saw (`fee_preview_conflict`). Writes the submission event and the fee snapshot in one transaction. Idempotent. |
| Fee | Fee rules under the pseudo category **REG** (`REG:brand_registration` 5,000.00 and `REG:agency_registration` 3,000.00, both synthetic, tax 0). REG is not in `master_category`, so it never appears in a model form or a rating scheme. `fee_rule_decide` accepts REG; the fee-rule service accepts a `*_registration` type only with REG. |
| Finance | `POST /api/registrations/{id}/fee-confirmation`: only Finance, never the payer's organisation or the person who submitted, the whole fee due within the payment tolerance, a receipt reference and a date; `fee_due` to `programme_review`. |
| Read | The owner's organisation; Finance at `fee_due`, Programme at `programme_review`, Director at `director_approval`. Everyone else gets `not_found` or `no_read_scope`. |

Nothing here writes to `brand` or `agency_authorisation`.
