# S2: QR batches and serials (local demonstration, not BEE rules)

- Real: tables `qr_batch`, `qr_serial` (serial unique), append-only `qr_event` (V54); public view `public_qr_serial` and runtime grants (V55); Spring module `qr` behind `/api/demo/qr` with role, organisation scope, state checks and an event row in the same transaction.
- Serial: `BEEQ-` + 10 Crockford base32 characters from a SecureRandom. Simulated: there is no printed label, scanner or real certificate authority; certificates are local demonstration certificates.
- Rules: manufacturer or agency of the application's organisation creates (quantity 1 to 5000, approved application with a certificate), allocates up to the quantity, binds, voids. Programme, admin, auditor read all. Another organisation's batch is `not_found`. Binding twice is idempotent; a duplicate in one request is refused ("Duplicate code blocked"); a serial of another batch, unknown or void is refused with a detail.
- Public check: `GET /api/public/verification?reg=BEEQ-...` resolves a BOUND serial to its certificate and answers in the usual shape; unknown, allocated, void and malformed all give the same 404. `/verify?reg=<serial>` works unchanged.
- Seed: `local/seed/demo/s2_qr.sql` (after `seed.sql`): two approved Nova models with certificates BEE/RAC/2026/10901 and 10902, two batches. Bound serials are `BEEQ-S2D0000001..25` and `BEEQ-S2D1000001..10`; `BEEQ-S2D0000040` is void; `BEEQ-S2D0000030` is allocated but not bound.
- Demo: nova.applicant opens QR batches, creates a batch, allocates, binds, voids one; "Verify this serial" opens the public check; the void or unbound serial shows not found; bee.programme sees all batches read-only; pixel.applicant sees none.
