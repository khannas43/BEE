# WP08.1b — The tax line on the fee (BL-132)

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `bl132-tax-line`; the full local gate passes (see Evidence). **Not independently reviewed or accepted.**

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner has no tax rule (4 October 2026) and asked for a recommended default, to be replaced from the client's rule. The default is recorded under decision A1 in [BEE_DECISIONS.md](../BEE_DECISIONS.md).

## What it does

| Rule | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| The rate | One rate in percent per fee-rule version (set in the fee-rule screen of WP08.1a). 0 means no tax is set. |
| The arithmetic | Tax = fee x rate / 100, rounded half up to the paisa. Whole fee due = fee + tax. The database derives tax and total from the amount and the rate (stored generated columns), so they cannot disagree with the amount; the portal's own arithmetic is tested to give the same figure. |
| The applicant | At the submit preview, after submit and in the application read, sees the fee, the tax with its rate, and the total. With no tax the text is unchanged ("₹24,000"). |
| Kept at submit | The rate is stored on the fee snapshot, so a later rule never changes a fee already given. Existing snapshots have rate 0 and total equal to the amount. |
| Finance | Confirms the **whole fee due** received: the amount must equal the total exactly (B12). With no tax the total equals the amount, so nothing changes. The queue and the detail show the fee due (total), the fee before tax and the tax. |
| The receipt | `amountInr` on the fee-confirmation receipt is the amount received, which is the total. |

## What changed

- **Flyway V34:** `tax_rate_percent`, and generated `tax_inr` and `total_inr`, on `model_application_fee_snapshot`.
- **Spring:** the fee rule carries its rate; the submit service captures it and shows `taxRatePercent`, `taxInr`, `totalInr`; Finance compares the amount received to the total.
- **Contract 0.18.0:** `SubmissionFee` gains the three fields; the fee-confirmation descriptions say the amount is the whole fee due.
- **Web:** `feeText`, the new-application and Finance screens, the fee-rule screen's note.
- **Docs:** decision A1 (the tax default), BL-132 done, BL-138 added.
