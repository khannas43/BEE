# WP08.1e — Correcting a fee confirmation (BL-133)

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `bl133-fee-correction`; the full local gate passes (see Evidence). **Not independently reviewed or accepted.**

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner's assumption for decision B11 (4 October 2026): manual confirmation by Finance **plus a correction route that needs a second Finance approver**.

## What it does

`/app/finance/receipt` ("Fee receipts", in the menu of anyone whose role holds the permission) lists the fifty most recent fee confirmations as first written, with any correction beside them, and lets authorised people correct the **receipt reference** or the **date received**:

| Rule | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Who | A person whose role holds the `fee_confirmation_correct` **permission** (Finance now; another role later by a database row). Everyone else gets 403 `role_not_permitted`. |
| Propose | The application, the right receipt reference (same rule as the confirmation), the right date received (not after today) and a reason. A correction that changes nothing is refused. |
| Approve or reject | A **different** holder; the proposer is refused (`segregation_refused`). Only the proposer may withdraw. |
| Separation | Nobody who belongs to the paying organisation, or who acted on the application at a stage other than the fee, may propose or decide (`segregation_refused`), even holding the permission. |
| One at a time | One correction waits per confirmation (`correction_already_pending`). |
| Never edited | The confirmation row is append-only and untouched. The values in effect are the latest approved correction; a second correction starts from the corrected values. The application history shows the correction beside the confirmation (the applicant sees the right values, not who approved). |

The refusals live in one database function, `fee_correction_decide`; the runtime login can add a proposal and run the function, nothing else. **Not modelled:** reversing a confirmation (BL-142); the amount (it must equal the fee due).
