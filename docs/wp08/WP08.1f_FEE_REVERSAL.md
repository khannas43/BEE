# WP08.1f — Reversing a fee confirmation recorded in error (BL-142)

**Status:** Implemented locally on branch `wp08-fee-reversal`; the full local gate passes (see Evidence). **Not independently reviewed or accepted.**

**Provisional local rules, not BEE rules.** The owner's assumption B17 ([BEE_DECISIONS.md](../BEE_DECISIONS.md)). A confirmation can be corrected ([WP08.1e](WP08.1e_FEE_CONFIRMATION_CORRECTION.md)) but not undone; this adds the undo for a confirmation that should never have been recorded.

## What it does

- **Who:** a person who holds the `fee_confirmation_correct` permission (Finance today) proposes the reversal with a reason (up to 500 characters); a **different** holder approves or rejects; the proposer may withdraw. Neither may belong to the paying organisation or have acted at another stage (the same separation as a correction). A reversal and a correction cannot wait on the same confirmation at once.
- **When:** only while nothing has happened to the application since the confirmation: it is in IAME scrutiny and the confirmation is the latest step, and that confirmation has not been reversed already. If anyone has started work (a recommendation, a return, a resubmission), proposing is refused (`reversal_not_possible`) and so is approving; the proposal stays, so it can be rejected. No officer's work is silently discarded (BL-152).
- **What approval does, in one transaction** (`fee_reversal_decide`, V40): the application goes from IAME scrutiny back to fee due (version + 1); a `reverse_fee` step is written to the history; the IAME officer's assignment is released; the applicant's organisation is notified that the fee is due again (the reason is Finance's internal note and is not in the text); the proposal is marked approved and tied to the step. The confirmation itself is never edited: it is marked reversed beside the proposal.
- **Confirming again:** Finance confirms the fee again as a new confirmation. The released assignment row is reactivated for the officer chosen (the old confirmation code inserted a new row and would have failed on the unique key; found by the database test). The confirmation in effect is the latest one that has not been reversed. A reversal step does not bar its approver from the fee stage.
- **History:** officers see the step with Finance's reason; the applicant sees the step ("Fee confirmation reversed; the fee is due again") without the reason or the officer's name.
- **Screen** (Fee receipts, `/app/finance/receipt`): a **Reverse** action on a confirmation still in IAME scrutiny with nothing waiting, a form for the reason, the reversals waiting for a second person and those recently decided (on the shared proposal panel), and a reversed confirmation shown as history with who reversed it.
- **Routes** (contract 0.26.0): `POST /api/fee-corrections/reversals`, `POST /api/fee-corrections/reversals/{id}/decision` (both with an Idempotency-Key) and the portal's matching routes; the existing read gains `reversalsPending`, `reversalsDecided` and, on each confirmation, `reversal` and `pendingReversalId`. New codes `reversal_already_pending` and `reversal_not_possible`; `reverse_fee` joins the history actions.
- **Insert guard (V40):** a reversal proposal is born pending; the runtime login cannot insert one that is already decided. The older proposal tables lack this guard (BL-151).

## Tests and evidence

- **Gate:** `npm run local:check` on the final commit: **1466 passed, 0 failed**, including `repo.lint-clean` (0 problems) and contract coverage (2303/2303 live observations conform; 429/429 internal and 587/587 browser pairs evidenced). It passed on the first full run.

- **Database** (`FeeCorrectionDatabaseTest`, 14 tests, 5 new): approval moves the application, releases the officer, writes the step, notifies without the reason, leaves the confirmation as written, and lets Finance confirm again (the new one is in effect; the old one cannot be reversed twice); the two-person and separation rules; refusal once anything has happened since (and one waiting per confirmation); the runtime login cannot edit, delete or insert a decided proposal; cleanup. Migration count 40 in every database test.
- **Spring contract** (`feeReversalOperationsDocumentedPairs`, plus a `reversal_already_pending` pair on correction proposals): every documented pair of both new routes and the extended read.
- **Web:** validators and client (`fee-corrections.test.mjs`), the screen (4 new cases), two `session_expired` unit-evidence tests.
- **Live** (`local:fee-corrections`, now 122 checks over two runs): Finance proposes in the portal (an empty reason is refused on screen), the same person cannot approve, a second reversal and a correction conflict while one waits, the applicant cannot propose, a different holder approves in the portal, the application is back at fee due with the officer released, one `reverse_fee` step, the confirmation untouched, the applicant is told (no reason), the officer reads the reason and the applicant does not, the reversed confirmation shows as history, Finance confirms again and the new one is in effect, a decided reversal cannot be decided twice, an unknown one is 404, a rejected one changes nothing, and the permission follows the role.
- **Not covered live:** the refusal after work has started (`reversal_not_possible`) is proven in the database and Spring contract tests and at the portal boundary with a stand-in; the live check has no IAME step in it.
